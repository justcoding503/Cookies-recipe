Instructions:

  1. Heat your oven to 170°C. Put baking paper on two trays.

  2. Mix the butter and both sugars with an electric whisk. Add the egg and vanilla, then mix again.

  3. Sift in the flour, baking powder, and salt. Mix everything together.

  4. Stir in the chocolate chips.

  5. Roll the dough into 24 small balls. Put them on the trays with space between each one.

  6. Bake for about 10 minutes until light brown. Cool and eat!
