Ingredients

Let's be real, we all skips the part of why every ingredient is important written in every recipe, so the only note you will need for this is recipe is to not forget the salt and vanilla! Key ingredients believe it or not, and now without further do, here's your ingredients:

The recipe in grams: or jump to the recipe in cups here

  • 125g softened butter
  • 50g brown sugar
  • 50g white sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • a splash of vanilla
  • 225g flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • a pinsh of salt
  • Chocolate chips according to taste

All purpose flour is the best for cookies, but if you want ultra-chewy cookies, then bread flour is you ideal choice! You can total substitute baking powder with baking soda if not avaible, and vanilla with maple syrup!

The recipe in cups: or go up to the recipe in grams here

  • 1/2 a cup softened butter
  • 1/4 of a cup brown sugar
  • 1/4 of a cup white sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • a splash of vanilla
  • 1 and 3/4 cups of flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • a pinsh of salt
  • Chocolate chips according to taste
